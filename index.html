My approach

How I design.

I ask questions, make the options tangible, and help the team decide what matters.

  1. 01 / Research

    Find the right problem.

    I start with how people work today. Their workarounds and hesitations help me question the brief and decide where design can help.

  2. 02 / Systems

    Set a shared direction.

    I map journeys and dependencies with product and engineering. Making the constraints visible helps us agree on priorities and understand the tradeoffs.

  3. 03 / AI prototyping

    Make ideas testable.

    I use AI to build working prototypes early. They give us something concrete to challenge, test and improve before we commit to a direction.

  4. 04 / Outcomes

    Follow through on the details.

    I test where people hesitate and refine the flow, language and interactions. I look for less effort, clearer decisions and fewer chances to go wrong.

The person behind the pixels

Curiosity is my starting point.

My path into product design took shape at NID, Bengaluru, where I studied Design for Retail Experience. Since then, I’ve worked across banking, logistics and cloud platforms. Each new domain has taught me to listen first and get comfortable asking questions.

Today, I’m a Lead Product Designer at IBM Cloud. Before that, I led design projects and mentored designers at Brillio, including work with VMware. I care about giving a team a clear direction, making the reasoning easy to follow, and staying close to the details as the work takes shape.

Away from product work, I draw, lift weights and tend to my plants. Illustration brings me back to observation: slowing down, noticing the small things and finding a story in what I see.
